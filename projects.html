Daily Grind Digital Cafe Menu

Project description

A mobile-friendly digital cafe menu designed to reduce repeated paper printing and plastic menu sleeves. Staff manage menu items, prices, and availability in a Google Sheet, and customers can view the current menu by scanning a QR code without installing an app.

  • Updates to the published Google Sheet feed the live menu webpage.
  • Customers can submit orders with their name and email, and save their name, email, and mobile number in the current browser for faster checkout.
  • WhatsApp updates are opt-in. The cafe emails UPI payment details and confirms payment after checking its bank app.
  • The menu is public; only people authorized by the cafe can edit the Google Sheet.

Started Oct 2026.
